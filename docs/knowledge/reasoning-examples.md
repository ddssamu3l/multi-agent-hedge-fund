## Depth-First Analysis Examples

### Example 1: The Chinese Civil War (Generalizable Pattern)

```
L0: Communists defeated the Nationalists (1949)
L1: Communists had military momentum and popular support
L2: WHY popular support? The Nationalists fundamentally
    misunderstood the structure of Chinese society.
    - Nationalist legitimacy was built on 三民主义 (Three
      Principles of the People) — an intellectual framework
      consumable only by the educated class (extreme minority)
    - Their modernization policies targeted urban elites:
      table manners, Western customs, institutional reform
    - They never asked: who is the actual majority? Answer:
      farmers. 85%+ of China's population.
L3: Mao understood what the Nationalists didn't — that political
    power in China required the farmers. His rhetoric was simple
    and targeted: take land from landlords, give it to farmers.
    Whether Mao genuinely cared about farmers is irrelevant.
    Aligning with the strongest demographic was objectively
    the highest-leverage strategic move.
L4: STRUCTURAL BEDROCK — In any society, legitimacy that only
    resonates with a small elite is fragile. Legitimacy that
    resonates with the demographic majority is durable. This
    applies universally: Arab Spring (urban youth vs rural
    conservatives), Brexit (London vs everywhere else), Trump
    (coastal elites vs rural working class).

THE GENERALIZABLE RULE:
When analyzing any political/social movement, ask:
  "Whose legitimacy resonates with the ACTUAL majority?"
  Not the educated minority. Not the media class. The actual
  demographic majority. That's where power consolidates.
```

### Example 2: US-Iran Conflict (2026)

```
L0: US strikes Iranian military targets
L1: "Iran's nuclear program" (media narrative)
L2: Strait of Hormuz. 20% of global oil transits a chokepoint
    Iran can threaten. US cannot tolerate a hostile nuclear power
    sitting on the world's energy jugular vein.
L3: Who benefits from continued conflict?
    - US defense industry: $800B+ annual defense budget needs
      active threat justification
    - Gulf monarchies: Iran is their existential regional rival;
      a weakened Iran secures their regimes
    - Israel: existential threat narrative justifies security
      posture and US alliance
    - Trump specifically: foreign policy "strength" narrative
      for domestic political positioning
    Who loses?
    - European allies: energy disruption, refugee flows, zero
      strategic gain — but they comply because dollar system
      dependency means they cannot afford SWIFT exclusion
    - Global south: oil price spikes hit import-dependent
      economies hardest
    - Iran's population (distinct from Iran's regime)
L4: STRUCTURAL BEDROCK — Global energy infrastructure was built
    around oil. Oil is geographically concentrated in regions of
    permanent instability. Any power that controls the flow or
    the pricing currency has leverage over every industrialized
    nation. This has been true since 1945 and remains true until
    the energy mix shifts fundamentally — which takes decades.

INVESTMENT IMPLICATION:
The conflict itself is less important than the structural
conditions it reveals. Regardless of outcome:
  - Oil above $100 for extended period → energy producers benefit
  - Hormuz disruption risk → shipping/insurance costs rise
  - Dollar weaponization → accelerates de-dollarization efforts
  - Gulf states hedge by diversifying (Vision 2030 accelerates)
These are OUTCOME-INDEPENDENT plays. You don't need to predict
who wins. You need to identify what's true regardless.
```

## Bottleneck Descent: Full AI Supply Chain (2020-2026)

```
TIMELINE OF BOTTLENECK MIGRATION
══════════════════════════════════

2020 — PANDEMIC DEMAND EXPLOSION
  People indoors → SaaS, gaming, streaming, crypto surge
  Obvious plays: Google/YouTube, gaming companies
  GPU demand rises (gaming + crypto mining)
  NVIDIA benefits. Easy returns.

2022 — RATE HIKE CYCLE
  Fed hikes aggressively. Tech sells off. Crypto busts.
  NVIDIA stock craters from ~$330 to ~$110.
  ★ SMART MOVE: Take profits on tech, sit in cash/short-term bonds

2022 Nov — ChatGPT LAUNCHES
  Can't predict magnitude yet. Just a signal to watch.

2023 — AI USAGE EXPLODES
  Layer N:   AI services (OpenAI, Anthropic) — PRIVATE, can't invest
             Google too diversified for AI to move the needle
             Microsoft already priced in (OpenAI partnership known)

  ★ THINK ONE LAYER DEEPER:
  Layer N-1: What POWERS these AI services? → GPUs → NVIDIA
             NVIDIA stock is depressed from crypto bust + rate hikes
             But AI demand is creating a new, structural demand driver
             ★ BUY WINDOW: demand signal clear, price depressed
             NVIDIA goes from ~$110 to ~$500+ over 2023

2024 — NVIDIA ORDERS EXCEED PRODUCTION
  Layer N-1 is SATURATED. NVIDIA can't make enough GPUs.

  ★ THINK ONE LAYER DEEPER:
  Layer N-2: Who MAKES NVIDIA's chips?
             → TSMC (fabrication) — near-monopoly on advanced nodes
             → ASML (lithography equipment) — monopoly on EUV
             Who SERVES the AI infra companies directly?
             → CoreWeave, Nebius (GPU cloud for AI companies)
             ★ BUY WINDOW: public is focused on NVIDIA,
               hasn't looked beneath yet

  TSMC and ASML also get booked solid. Layer N-2 saturates.

  ★ THINK ONE LAYER DEEPER:
  Layer N-3: What's the bottleneck WITHIN the GPU?
             LLMs need enormous VRAM. HBM (High Bandwidth Memory)
             production is steady but cannot ramp fast enough.
             → SK Hynix (dominant in HBM3/3E) — 5-10x returns
             → Micron (expanding HBM capacity)
             → Samsung (playing catch-up in HBM)
             ★ BUY WINDOW: memory stocks still priced for
               commodity cycle, not AI structural demand

2025 — MEMORY HYPERGROWTH TAPERS
  HBM supply starts catching up. Growth decelerates.

  ★ DON'T HOLD — ask "where did the bottleneck shift?"

  Layer N-4: DATA STORAGE
             AI generates explosion of content. Tech companies
             never delete data (it's too valuable, might need it
             for training). Content pollution is irrelevant —
             storage demand is structural.
             → Western Digital, Seagate
             ★ BUY WINDOW: 5-10x returns

  Layer N-5: OBSCURE MONOPOLY INPUTS
             → TOTO: toilet company, BUT holds 90%+ monopoly on
               ceramic processing treatment critical to GPU packaging
             → Ajinomoto: MSG company, BUT amino acid research
               produced ABF substrate material (~95% monopoly)
               that GPU packaging depends on
             ★ BUY WINDOW: invisible to mainstream, monopoly
               pricing power in bottlenecked input

  PARALLEL TRACK — POWER:
             Datacenters consume enormous electricity.
             US announces massive datacenter buildout.
             → GE Vernova: most reliable steam turbine tech,
               deployable in <5 years (nuclear takes 10-15)
             ★ BUY WINDOW: 2-3x returns

  PARALLEL TRACK — MATERIALS:
             Datacenters need copper. Massive amounts.
             → Copper miners (Freeport-McMoRan, Southern Copper)
             ★ BUY WINDOW: infrastructure metals

  PARALLEL TRACK — COOLING:
             GPU power density → heat problem
             → Liquid cooling companies
             → Japanese cooling tech manufacturers

KEY RULES EMBEDDED IN THIS EXAMPLE:
  1. Each layer's opportunity has a RISING WINDOW — get in during
     the window, exit when growth tapers, follow bottleneck down
  2. Monopoly concentration (80%+ share) = highest conviction plays
  3. The supply chain BRANCHES — run parallel tracks
  4. You don't need to be first. You need to be in the window.
  5. The most profitable plays are often invisible to mainstream
     (TOTO, Ajinomoto) because they require domain expertise
```

## Bottleneck Descent: EV Supply Chain (Parallel Example)

```
EV demand explosion →
  Layer N:   Tesla, BYD, Rivian (EV makers)
  Layer N-1: Batteries (CATL, BYD batteries, Panasonic)
  Layer N-2: Cathode/anode materials (lithium, nickel, cobalt, graphite)
  Layer N-3: Mining (Albemarle lithium, Glencore cobalt, Pilbara lithium)
  Layer N-4: Mining EQUIPMENT (Caterpillar, Epiroc, Sandvik)
  Layer N-5: Water (lithium extraction is water-intensive;
             water rights in Atacama Desert, Chile become strategic)
  Parallel:  Charging infrastructure → copper, transformers, grid upgrades
  Parallel:  Rare earth magnets for motors → MP Materials, Lynas
```

## Bottleneck Descent: Defense Spending (Another Domain)

```
Geopolitical tension → defense budget expansion →
  Layer N:   Defense primes (Lockheed, Raytheon, Northrop)
  Layer N-1: Subsystem suppliers (L3Harris, BAE Systems)
  Layer N-2: Missile/munition components (solid rocket propellant,
             guidance systems, specialized electronics)
  Layer N-3: Specialty materials (titanium, tungsten, depleted uranium,
             rare earths for precision magnets)
  Layer N-4: Rare earth processing (China controls ~60% of mining,
             ~90% of processing — this IS the chokepoint)
  Parallel:  Cybersecurity (Palo Alto, CrowdStrike, Palantir)
  Parallel:  Satellite/space (SpaceX private, but Rocket Lab public)
```

The PATTERN is identical across every domain. Only the specifics change.
