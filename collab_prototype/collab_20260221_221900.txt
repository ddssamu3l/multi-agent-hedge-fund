I want this to feel like the saddest song you can still play with the windows down.

**Core concept (story):**
Two best friends in the same summer town, but living in different timelines now. Nobody “did” anything—life just kept moving, and suddenly the friendship is a faded tan line. The ache is in the small stuff: unread texts, inside jokes that don’t land, a beach you both still go to but never at the same time.

**Emotional tone:**
Bittersweet + resigned, not dramatic. The grief is quiet but persistent—like heat at 9pm that won’t let go. The chorus should hit like a wave: you’re dancing, but you’re crying a little.

**Sound / genre direction (for you, Kai):**
Art-pop / alt-pop with a pop spine: glossy enough for anthem, weird enough to feel true.
- Bright, sun-bleached synths + a warm bassline that feels like skin.
- Drums that suggest summer (four-on-the-floor or syncopated R&B bounce), but with a hollowed-out mix—like the party is down the street.
- A “drop” that isn’t EDM, more like an emotional lift: vocal stack + synth bloom + percussion snaps.

**Vocal + topline vibe (my lane):**
Intimate verses (half-spoken, confessional), then a chorus that opens up and repeats a simple, devastating line. I want one hook that people can scream-sing, but it has to be poetic, not generic.

**Lyrical imagery / motifs:**
- Sunscreen / salt / melted ice / car doors slamming / flip-phone nostalgia or old photos
- “Late July” energy: everything looks golden but you feel the ending
- Inside jokes as artifacts; shared spots as haunted locations
- Time stamps: 2:13am voice note, August 6th, last day of school, last swim

**Structure idea (unusual but still pop):**
- Verse 1 (specific memory)  
- Pre (realization: we’re not syncing anymore)  
- Chorus (anthem line, repeated)  
- Verse 2 (present day: we’re polite strangers)  
- Chorus  
- Bridge (voice-note / fragmented memories, almost spoken)  
- Final chorus (bigger, with a gut-punch extra line)

**Potential chorus hook candidates (pick one lane):**
1) “It’s still our summer, just not ours.”  
2) “Same sun, different shadows.”  
3) “I miss you in the middle of the song.”

**Production lane: A) R&B-leaning alt-pop.**  
That’s the only lane that naturally holds *quiet grief* in the verses while still letting the chorus go full windows-down. The Frank-ish restraint gives us intimacy (half-spoken, details, negative space), and then we can let the chorus bloom Lorde-style—wide, sun-bleached, communal—without turning it into melodrama. It’ll feel like body-heat at 9pm: smooth, sticky, impossible to shake.

**Why not B or C:**  
- **B (indie electronic)** risks getting “cool-sad” and textural, but not scream-sing. Great for headphones, less for car speakers.  
- **C (straight pop banger)** is tempting, but it’ll flatten the nuance—your concept needs *air* and *absence* as part of the groove.

---

### Hook lane (I’m picking): **#1 “It’s still our summer, just not ours.”**
That line is devastating and *simple enough to chant*, but still poetic. It also contains the whole thesis: the town is the same, the season is the same, but the ownership is gone. #2 is pretty but more abstract; #3 is cool but slightly “songwriter-y” and less universal.

We can still weave #2 and #3 in as supporting lines (pre/bridge), but the chorus should anchor on #1.

---

### Ending: **Unresolved (but with a soft, resigned aftertaste).**
Not “I’m spiraling,” but “I keep checking my phone even though I already know.” That’s the truth of growing apart: there’s no villain, so your brain keeps trying to solve it like a puzzle. The final chorus can add one gut-punch line that *admits the habit*.

Think: last hook repeats like you’re trying to convince yourself it’s fine… while you’re not.

---

### Sonic north star (so you can write into it)
- **Verses:** minimal, close-mic vocal; warm sub/bass that feels like skin; little percussive ticks like keys/flip-flop snaps; a synth that sounds sun-faded (slightly detuned, “bleached”).  
- **Pre:** drums start to “walk forward,” harmonies creep in like memory.  
- **Chorus lift (not EDM):** vocal stack + synth bloom + claps/snaps + a brighter chord change that feels like stepping into golden hour—then the hollow hits: a quick drop of low-end for one beat like the stomach falling.
