--- Round 1/8 | Lyra ---

Vision: a **sun-bleached banger that hurts**—the kind of song you can scream with the windows down and then go quiet on the last chorus because it’s too accurate.

### Core concept (story + metaphor)
Two best friends in the same summer, but on different timelines. You’re still trying to “loop” the old version of you two, while they’re already buffering into a new life.

**Central metaphor:** *summer as a dying battery / evaporating pool water / a tape warping in the heat.*  
Everything’s bright, but it’s slipping through your fingers.

### Emotional tone
**Bittersweet > tragic.**  
Not a betrayal, not a fight. Just the slow horror of realizing you’re becoming “someone I used to know” to the person who knew you best.

### Genre + palette (so it can be an anthem)
Art-pop/alt-pop spine with Kai-friendly pop/R&B/electronic muscle:
- **Shimmery, warm production** (like heat haze)
- Under it: **a colder, hollow sub-bass** (the distance)
- Percussion that feels like: **pool parties + heart monitor** (four-on-the-floor-ish but not EDM)
- A hook that’s **simple enough to chant**, but the verses are **poetic and specific**

### Song structure (unusual but still pop)
- **Verse 1:** golden memories in sharp detail (sensory, specific)
- **Pre:** the first “uh-oh” moment—when you notice the lag
- **Chorus:** the thesis, big and singable
- **Verse 2:** present day—unreturned texts, different crowds, algorithmic friendship
- **Bridge:** the emotional gut-punch: admitting you miss the old “us” more than you miss *them* as they are now
- **Final chorus:** bigger, then a sudden drop to almost nothing on the last line

### Hook idea (directional, not final)
A chorus that lands on a line like:
- “We’re still the same in photos”  
- “Your laugh is getting farther away”  
- “It’s a long goodbye in sunglasses”  
- “I’m waving from the shallow end”

### Lyrical rules I want us to follow
- No generic “we drifted apart” lines.
- Give me **one signature summer object** we return to (pool, cheap sunscreen, corner store ice, car stereo, fireworks).
- Keep the language modern but not cringey—*one* tech reference max (e.g., read receipts) and make it poetic.

### Question for Kai (to lock the sound)
Do you want this to lean:
1) **Dancey melancholy** (Lorde “Green Light” energy: hurt but moving), or  
2) **Slow-burn nocturnal** (Frank Ocean-ish: glossy and restrained), or  
3) **Indie-electronic lift** (Radiohead-ish texture with a pop topline)?

If you answer that, I’ll generate a first full lyric draft with a chorus we can actually build a record around.
